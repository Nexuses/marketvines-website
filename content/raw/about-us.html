“Dr. Brad” is a Digital Transformation growth and leadership expert, with decades of practical leadership experience applying market intelligence and growing technology firms. He an executive presence and experienced speaker on intersection of markets and emergent technology at events like CES, AI & Big Data Expo, IoT Evolution, IPVM, Pubcon, and Sensors Expo, as well as broadcast media /podcasts including IoT Now, Democratic Visions, EPLN, Next Curve, Transforma Insights, and Conversations in Applied AI.
